Furniture AI · Ken Tam
Furniture AI reads a published floor plan, lays out draft furniture, fills each piece with a dated product from a checked page within one budget, and exports a checklist and spreadsheet. Runtime model: gpt-6-luna only, via Portkey.

The problem

Furnishing a rented apartment you've barely seen

  • You have a marketing floor plan, a budget and a style, but no trusted measurements.
  • Prices and sizes are spread across many store tabs and go stale quickly.
  • One expensive piece quietly breaks the budget for the rest of the apartment.
  • It's hard to tell which facts were actually checked, and when.
Furniture AI · Ken Tam
No market statistics are claimed. The motivation is the renter's uncertainty and tab sprawl; the app aims for one consistent, budgeted, sourced list that says what is approximate.

What is new

A plan-first, budget-honest combination

  • IKEA Kreativ, Planner 5D and Cona already plan rooms; Cona overlaps closely, with priced products.
  • Furniture AI starts from the building's published plans (19 at The Archive) and covers the whole apartment.
  • Uncertainty is shown, products carry check dates, and code enforces one budget across stores.

Safety

Defenses enforced in code, tested

  • Pages are untrusted; models only fill typed schemas.
  • SSRF guard: public DNS only, pinned connection, every redirect checked.
  • Source allowlist (not a seller guarantee).
  • Server refuses choices over budget; token caps.
